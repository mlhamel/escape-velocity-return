# PvP Policy (MMO)

[stable] — decided in ADR-0008

Government zones are the ruleset:

- **Policed space** (Confederation core, etc.): attacking players brings
  instant NPC response and faction penalty. Possible, never profitable.
- **Contested / pirate / lawless systems**: open PvP. No warning, no guards.
- **Hyperlanes**: TBD — origin-system rules or destination rules apply

Design intent: EV's government/faction logic literally becomes the PvP
design. Player pirates get real space to be pirates; traders get real risk
arcs.
