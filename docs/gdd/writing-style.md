# Writing Style

[active] — applies to every string in `content/`

Escape Velocity's secret weapon was sharp writing. Planet descriptions and
mission briefings had real tone. Keep that.

Rules:

1. Dry wit over epic bombast. A planet blurb is a short story, not a brochure.
2. Specific nouns. "The skyline bazaars of Nairobi Orbital" beats "bustling
   markets".
3. Briefings are written by characters — bureaucrats sound bureaucratic,
   smugglers sound like smugglers.
4. Never explain the joke; never wink at the player.
5. Under 60 words for a description, under 120 for a briefing.
